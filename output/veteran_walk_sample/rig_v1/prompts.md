# Veteran rig part prompts

Built-in imagegen was used with `assets/sprites/idle_veteran.png` as the character
and style reference.

## Upper body

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 2D game cutout rig upper-body atlas.
Create an EXACT 4 columns by 2 rows atlas, eight equal square cells, landscape
2:1 canvas. Each cell contains the SAME muscular gray-bearded veteran commando
UPPER BODY ONLY, cut cleanly at the hips. He holds exactly ONE large olive-and-
black military machine gun in BOTH hands at chest height and aims in the view
direction. Direction order left-to-right: top S front, SW front-left, W left
profile, NW rear-left; bottom N rear, NE rear-right, E right profile, SE
front-right. Preserve the long flowing red headband tails, short gray hair, full
gray beard where visible, green tactical vest and pouches, tan sleeveless shirt,
huge bare muscular arms, black fingerless gloves, and chunky five-head-tall
proportions. Rear views show the back of the head, headband, vest and shoulders,
not a face. The machine gun must remain identical in every view. Match the
reference North American action cartoon style, bold continuous black outlines,
hard-edge three-tone cel shading, saturated slightly grimy palette. Same scale,
hip anchor at 84% cell height, centered body, generous gun and headband margins.
Uniform solid #FF00FF background. NO LEGS, NO BOOTS, no second weapon, muzzle
flash, labels, dividers, shadow, checkerboard or gradients. This is a rigid torso
atlas, not animation frames.

## Leg parts

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 2D game cutout rig leg-parts atlas.
Create EXACTLY THREE equal square cells in one horizontal row on uniform solid
#FF00FF. Match the veteran commando's very dark olive-brown cargo pants and
massive black combat boots, bold continuous black outlines, and hard-edge
three-tone cel shading. LEFT: one isolated very wide muscular cargo-pants thigh,
straight vertical, rounded hip connector at top and knee connector at bottom,
extra full at the hip so two thighs connect naturally under the veteran's broad
tactical belt and massive torso, with a cargo pocket and fabric folds, tapering
moderately toward knee. MIDDLE: one isolated thick dark cargo-pants lower leg and
boot shaft, straight vertical, rounded knee and ankle connectors, armored boot
cuff and fabric folds. RIGHT: one isolated massive black combat-boot foot in
right profile, ankle opening top-left, heel left, reinforced rounded toe right,
thick rugged sole. Center and widely separate parts, occupying about 65% of each
cell. NO whole character, skin, arms, gun, overlapping pieces, text, dividers,
shadow, checkerboard or gradients.

## Directional boots

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 2D game cutout rig directional combat-
boot atlas. Create EXACT 4 columns by 2 rows, eight equal square cells. Each cell
contains ONE identical massive black military COMBAT-BOOT FOOT ONLY, from ankle
downward, turned in top-row S, SW, W, NW and bottom-row N, NE, E, SE order. Toe
extends in the stated direction. Preserve black and charcoal leather, armored
rounded toe cap, broad ankle opening, panel seams, laces where visible, very
thick rugged tread sole, bold continuous black outline, hard-edge three-tone cel
shading matching the veteran reference. Constant scale, sole baseline at 76%
cell height, ankle anchor x50% y38%; side views about 65% cell width and
front/back about 38%. Uniform solid #FF00FF. ONE boot per cell; NO pairs, leg,
shin, protruding connector, body, weapon, text, grid, shadow, checkerboard or
gradients.
