# Engineer rig part prompts

Built-in imagegen was used with `assets/sprites/idle_engineer.png` as the character
and style reference.

## Upper body

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 2D game cutout rig upper-body atlas.
Create an EXACT 4 columns by 2 rows atlas, eight equal square cells, landscape
2:1 canvas. Each cell contains the SAME young field engineer UPPER BODY ONLY,
cut cleanly at the hips. He holds exactly ONE industrial nail gun in BOTH hands
at chest height and aims in the view direction. Direction order left-to-right:
top S front, SW front-left, W left profile, NW rear-left; bottom N rear, NE
rear-right, E right profile, SE front-right. Preserve a round dome-shaped yellow
hard hat with short brim and goggles resting on top, compact young face, rolled
cream shirt sleeves, brown overall chest bib and shoulder straps, brown work
gloves, and chunky five-head-tall proportions. Rear views show the back of the
hard hat, straps and shoulders, not a face. The compact gray-and-yellow nail gun
must remain the same in every view. Match the reference North American action
cartoon style, bold continuous black outlines, hard-edge three-tone cel shading,
and saturated slightly grimy palette. Same scale, hip anchor at 84% cell height,
centered body, generous gun margins. Uniform solid #FF00FF background. NO LEGS,
NO BOOTS, no backpack, wrench, second weapon, labels, dividers, shadow,
checkerboard or gradients. This is a rigid torso atlas, not animation frames.

## Leg parts

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 2D game cutout rig leg-parts atlas.
Create EXACTLY THREE equal square cells in one horizontal row on solid #FF00FF.
Match the engineer's brown overalls and oversized work boots, bold black outlines,
and hard-edge three-tone cel shading. LEFT: one isolated wide brown-overall thigh,
straight vertical, rounded hip connector at top and knee connector at bottom,
very full and baggy at the hip so two thighs attach naturally under the broad
overall waist, tapering moderately toward the knee. MIDDLE: one isolated wide
brown work-boot shin/shaft, straight vertical, rounded knee and ankle connectors,
oversized folded cuff, leather panels and straps. RIGHT: one isolated chunky
brown work-boot foot in right profile, ankle opening top-left, heel left, large
reinforced toe right, thick dark sole. Center and widely separate the parts,
occupying about 65% of each cell. No whole character, overlapping pieces, text,
dividers, shadow or checkerboard.

## Directional boots

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 2D game cutout rig directional boot atlas.
Create EXACT 4 columns by 2 rows, eight equal square cells. Each contains ONE
identical oversized brown industrial WORK-BOOT FOOT ONLY, from ankle downward,
turned in top-row S, SW, W, NW and bottom-row N, NE, E, SE order. Toe extends in
the stated direction. Preserve the heavy brown leather, reinforced rounded toe,
wide ankle opening, thick dark sole, bold black outline and hard-edge three-tone
cel shading. Constant scale, sole baseline at 76% cell height, ankle anchor x50%
y38%; side views about 65% cell width and front/back about 38%. Solid #FF00FF.
ONE boot per cell; no pairs, leg, shin, protruding connector, text, grid, shadow
or checkerboard.
