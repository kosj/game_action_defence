## Torso atlas

Create a CUTOUT SKELETAL ANIMATION PARTS ATLAS, not an animation. Exactly 4 columns by 2 rows, eight equal square cells, landscape 2:1 canvas. Each cell contains the SAME green hooded crossbow huntress UPPER BODY ONLY, cut off at belt/hips. NO LEGS, NO BOOTS. Includes head, hood, ponytail, torso, both arms and hands gripping ONE crossbow, short green cape ending at belt level. Preserve reference bold black comic outlines, leather brown bodice, charcoal waist, green hood, single black ponytail, 3 flat tones. Constant size about 75 percent of cell height, body centered, belt endpoint at 85 percent cell height, plenty of margin around crossbow. Cell directions left to right: top row SOUTH front, SOUTHWEST front-left, WEST left profile, NORTHWEST back-left; bottom row NORTH back, NORTHEAST back-right, EAST right profile, SOUTHEAST front-right. Each view anatomically turned, not same direction. For S point crossbow toward camera with foreshortening; N point it away with cape hiding hands. Uniform flat magenta #FF00FF background for runtime chroma key. No check pattern, no shadow, no grid lines, no labels. These are rigid torso attachments for a 2 bone leg rig; ABSOLUTELY NO LEGS below the brown belt. All bodies same scale, identity and costume.

## Leg atlas

A 2D game CUTOUT RIG PARTS texture atlas, exactly THREE equal square cells in a single horizontal row. Uniform solid magenta #FF00FF background, no text, no divider lines, no shadows. Match reference huntress comic art, thick dark black outlines, 3-tone flat cel shading. LEFT CELL: one isolated charcoal trouser THIGH segment, straight VERTICAL from round hip joint at top to round knee joint at bottom, thick tapered cloth shape, no torso no foot. MIDDLE CELL: one isolated brown leather boot SHAFT / shin segment, straight VERTICAL from round knee at top to ankle at bottom, warm medium brown, folded wide boot cuff near top, leather straps, no foot. RIGHT CELL: one isolated brown leather boot FOOT only in right-facing side view, ankle opening at top-left with short rounded connector, heel at left, round chunky toe at right, dark brown thick flat sole. Parts separated widely, no overlapping, no whole character. Each piece occupies center 65 percent of its equal square cell. The thigh and shin are rigid 2D bone attachments with rounded end caps so joints overlap smoothly. This is a game rig source texture, not an illustration.
